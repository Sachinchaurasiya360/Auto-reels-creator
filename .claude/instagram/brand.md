# Channel brand

**Current (series, since 2026-10-31):** the InternHack series look. Source of truth is
`series/studio/src/series/brand.ts` and `series/PLAN.md` §1:

- Warm off-white ground `#fafaf9` / `#f5f5f4` with a drifting dot grid, ink `#0c0a09`, muted `#57534e`,
  border `#e7e5e4`
- Accent: logo blue `#0074ff`; lime `#a3e635` (ink `#3f6212`) only for success / "it worked"; red `#ef4444`
  failure; amber `#f59e0b` waiting
- Fonts: Space Grotesk (display), Inter (body), JetBrains Mono (code)
- Series badge top-centre (logo + series name + Day/Ep); Roman Hinglish word captions under it; content in
  the 560-1320 px band; nothing important below 1250 px (platform UI)
- No photos or AI images: drawn UI, phones, devices, avatars

**Retired (pre-series reels, before 2026-10-31):** navy `#0f1626` with cream text, orange `#ff9a1f` accent,
Archivo Black / Montserrat 900 captions. Don't use it for new reels.
